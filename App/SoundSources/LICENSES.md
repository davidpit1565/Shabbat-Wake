## What we changed

Files were downloaded from OpenGameArt, converted to mono MP3 (44.1 kHz, 96 kbps), given a very short fade in and a 0.4 s fade out,
and cut to at most 30 s where the original was longer (Morning Chime, Music Box tracks, Meadow Birds, Wind and Birds, Marimba Morning).
Nothing else was changed. `App/AlarmSoundGenerator.py` loops each one to the ring length the person chose.

## Checked and not used

- Pages whose license was CC-BY or inconsistent between listings (for example Calm Piano 1, Pleasant Creek, Morning Sky, a kalimba track).
- Anything on Freesound (downloads need a login, so license and file could not be checked here), Pixabay and BigSoundBank.
