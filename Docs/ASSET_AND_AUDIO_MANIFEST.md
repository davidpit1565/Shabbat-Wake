# Asset and Audio Manifest

No production audio tracks or final icon artwork have been supplied or verified in this repository yet. Do not ship placeholders as finished assets.

## Required original/cleared sound set
- Gentle chime — short confirmation and optional wake-up tone.
- Soft piano — wake-up tone.
- Ambient dawn — wake-up tone.
- Traditional-style instrumental — only if composition and recording rights are explicitly cleared.
- Silent/vibration option — subject to the capabilities and rules of the native alarm API.
- Preview clip for each selectable sound, with accessible labels and a stop control.

For every bundled audio file, record filename, duration, format, loudness normalization, composer/performer, source, license/permission, territory, term, attribution requirements, and proof of rights. Do not bundle recordings of copyrighted songs or commercially released melodies without a license. “Found online” is not a license.

## App icon and visual assets
- Final 1024×1024 App Store icon source, opaque where required.
- Xcode AppIcon asset catalog compatible with the chosen Xcode/iOS SDK.
- Small-size legibility review and contrast review.
- App Store screenshots generated from the actual release candidate, not the concept mockup.
- Any font, image, illustration, or sound must have a recorded license/source.
